# Users and roles

The **Users** area lists the people who can sign in to the back office.

## Roles

- **Admin** — full access, including approvals, project management, settings, and user administration.
- **Seller** — works the sales pipeline; cannot approve payments/contracts or change settings.
- **User** — a customer account. Customers use the separate customer portal.

## What each role can do here

- **Sellers** can view the user list and create users.
- **Admins** can also delete, activate/deactivate, and restore users.

## User status

| Status | Meaning |
| --- | --- |
| Active | Can sign in. |
| Inactive | Cannot sign in. |
| Suspended | Blocked, for example after repeated failures. |

## Password requirements

When creating a user, the password must have at least **8 characters**, one **uppercase** letter, one **lowercase** letter, one **number**, and one **special character**.

## Good practice

- Use the **seller** role for sales staff and **admin** only for people who must approve money or change settings.
- Deactivate a user instead of deleting when you may need the history later.
