# Approving, rejecting, and reopening contracts

> **Admin only.** These actions change money and lot availability.

## Approve

Approving a contract confirms the sale and **generates the payment schedule**. Only contracts in **pending**, **submitted**, or **rejected** status can be approved.

Before approving, confirm:

- the customer and lot are correct,
- the reservation and down payment amounts match the agreement,
- the financing type and payment term are correct,
- for bank or cash financing, the maximum payment date is set.

## Reject

Rejecting requires a reason. The customer is notified and the lot returns to **available**. A rejected contract can be corrected and submitted again, or deleted.

## Cancel

Cancelling stops the sale before approval. Like rejection, it releases the lot. Use cancellation when the customer walks away. The cancellation is recorded in the contract notes.

## Reopen

A **cancelled** or **closed** contract can be reopened. It returns to **approved** and payments can continue. Reopen only when a closing or cancellation was a mistake or a refund/reversal is needed.

## Delete a rejected contract

A rejected contract can be deleted so the lot can be reserved again. This is different from cancelling: deleting removes the record. The row menu shows **Delete** only for rejected contracts.
