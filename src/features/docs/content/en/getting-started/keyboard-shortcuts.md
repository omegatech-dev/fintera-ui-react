# Keyboard shortcuts

Shortcuts use a chord: press **g** to navigate or **n** to create, then the indicated key. The second key must be pressed within about 1.2 seconds; otherwise the chord expires and nothing happens.

Press **?** at any time to open the shortcut list for your role. Close it with **Escape**.

## When shortcuts are paused

Shortcuts never interrupt typing. They are ignored when the focus is inside an input, a text area, a select, or any editable field, and when you hold **Ctrl**, **Cmd**, or **Alt**. They also stay inactive while another dialog is open.

Hover or focus a sidebar entry to see its chord next to the label.
