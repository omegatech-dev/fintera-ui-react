# System overview and roles

Fintera is a back office for real-estate financing. It tracks **projects**, their **lots**, the **contracts** that sell those lots, and the **payment schedule** that collects the money.

## Main areas

| Area | What it is for |
| --- | --- |
| Projects | Create and publish developments. |
| Lots | Track availability and pricing per lot. |
| Contracts | Sell or reserve lots and follow approval. |
| Payments | Review receipts and collect installments. |
| Reports | Export commissions, revenue, and overdue lists. |
| Settings | Configure branding and general options (admins only). |

## Roles

- **Admin** — full access: approves contracts and payments, and manages projects, lots, users, settings, and branding.
- **Seller** — works the sales pipeline: views projects and lots, creates reservations, views contracts and payments, uploads receipts, and reads reports. Sellers cannot approve or reject contracts or payments, and cannot change settings.
- **Customer** — uses the separate customer portal, not this back office.

> Your role controls which articles appear in this guide. Sellers only see the operations they can perform.
