# System overview and roles

This article describes the seller workspace. Your role focuses on the sales pipeline: reserving lots for clients, following their contracts, registering collection receipts, and checking your own commissions.

## Your menu

Sellers see these entries in the sidebar:

| Entry | What it is for |
| --- | --- |
| **My day** | Your home page: sales summary, activity, referral link, and quick actions. |
| **My link** | Your public referral link, QR code, and attributed reservations. |
| **My clients** | Clients from your contracts and historical clients you add. |
| **Collections** | Contracts with a balance and the receipt upload flow. |
| **Commissions** | Your commissions for the current month, read-only. |
| **Documents** | Contracts with documents available and account statements. |
| **Guide** | This guide. |

## Create a reservation

1. Select **New reservation** on **My day**, or open **Projects** from a direct link.
2. Choose an available lot.
3. In the **Client** step, search for an existing customer or create a new one with name, phone, identity, RTN, and email.
4. In the **Financing plan** step, choose direct, bank, or cash financing and complete the payment terms.
5. Review the summary, attach any documents, and confirm.

The lot moves to **reserved** and the contract starts in **pending** status. The seller can attach the reservation voucher and follow the contract, but cannot approve, reject, or cancel it.

## Add and follow clients

- **My clients** lists the customers from your own contracts. Use the search box to filter by name, phone, or identity.
- **Add client** opens the historical client form. Use it to register a client and sale that happened before you started using Fintera. Until an admin validates it, the record appears as **Historical · pending validation**.
- **Import CSV** loads several historical clients at once from a spreadsheet.

New customers for an active sale are created inside the reservation wizard.

## Share your referral link

**My link** gives you a public link, a QR code, and a WhatsApp share action. Every reservation completed through that link is assigned to you automatically and generates a commission based on the project rate.

When a reservation is assigned to you, the notifications bell shows an acceptance card with a countdown. Select **Accept** before the countdown expires; accepted reservations appear in **My clients** and in your commissions.

## Register collections

1. Open **Collections** and select **Collect** on a contract with a pending installment.
2. Review the client, installment, due date, amount, and current balance.
3. Upload the receipt as an image or PDF.
4. Confirm **Register collection**.

The payment moves to **In review** and the balance does not change until an admin approves it. Sellers cannot approve, reject, or undo payments.

## Check commissions

**Commissions** shows your commissions for the current month in read-only mode. Admins manage commission rates, exceptions, and payouts.

## Documents and contracts

- **Documents** lists your contracts with documents available and downloads the customer account statement directly.
- Selecting a contract opens its detail in read-only mode. You can review the schedule, payments, documents, and notes, download the contract PDF, and view the lot.

## What you cannot do

Sellers cannot:

- approve, reject, cancel, or reopen contracts;
- approve, reject, or undo payments;
- manage projects, lots, media, or payment instructions;
- manage users, settings, branding, or terms;
- change commission rates or pay commissions;
- open Analytics or Audits.
