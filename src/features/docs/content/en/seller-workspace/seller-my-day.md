# My day

**My day** is the seller's home page. It opens automatically after sign-in and summarizes the seller's sales activity.

> Admins can read this article, but their home page is the admin Dashboard.

## Header

The page greets the seller by name, shows today's date, and offers two shortcuts:

- **New reservation** — opens Projects so the seller can choose an available lot.
- **Create client** — opens the historical client form.

## Summary cards

- **Sales this month** — the latest value in the six-month sales trend. The caption shows the six-month total.
- **Estimated commission** — commission pending payment.
- **Active leads** — leads that are pending or in review.

## Recent activity

The activity card lists the latest updated contracts and clients with name, project, status chip, amount, and date. **View all** opens **My clients**.

## Commission detail

This card shows the pending commission, the conversion rate with a progress bar, and the explanation that the rate is the share of leads that became approved sales. **View commissions** opens a commission detail modal for the current month.

## Performance trend

A line chart of sales over the last six months. If there are no approved sales yet, the card explains that the chart appears once a sale is approved.

## What the seller cannot do here

The seller workspace intentionally hides Contracts, Projects and Settings from the sidebar. Actions that change money or configuration remain admin-only.
